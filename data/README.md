# data/

Put the data files from **Moodle** into this folder:

| File | Content | Tutorial |
|------|---------|----------|
| `compustat.csv` | Compustat North America, fiscal years 2010–2014 | 1, 2 |
| `compustat_German_format.csv` | same data, German CSV format | 1 |
| `crsp_ccm_link.csv` | CRSP/Compustat Merged link table | 2 |
| `crsp_msf_2009_2015.csv` | CRSP monthly stock file, 2009–2015 | 2 |
| `compustat_global_2023_2025.csv` | Compustat Global fundamentals, fiscal years 2023–2025 | 2 |
| `hail_raw_firm_years.csv.gz` | Worldscope raw data 1999–2008 for Hail, Tahoun and Wang (2014), merged and matched (country, IFRS year, SIC, US cross-listing) | 5 |
| `hail_raw_dividends_1997_2008.csv.gz` | Worldscope dividends per share and total assets, 1997–2008 | 5 |

The SRN files (`srn_*.csv`) are downloaded by your own code in Tutorial 2.

All files are **licensed data** (WRDS) or downloaded with your personal token. They are listed in
`.gitignore` and are therefore never uploaded to GitHub. Do not share them outside the course and
do not paste them into AI tools.
